# AI Free Tools Ecosystem

Version: 1.0.0

## 1. Revenue Streams

### Stream 1: Display Advertising (Primary — 60% of revenue)

**Ad Networks (tier progression):**

| Stage | Network | RPM Target | Traffic Required |
|-------|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| Launch | Google AdSense | $5–$8 | Any |
| Growth | Ezoic | $10–$15 | 10K+ sessions/month |
| Scale | Mediavine | $15–$25 | 50K sessions/month |
| Enterprise | Raptive (AdThrive) | $20–$40 | 100K sessions/month |

**Ad placement strategy:**
- Above tool form (header leaderboard)
- Between FAQ sections (in-content)
- Sidebar (desktop only)
- After generation result (highest CTR position)
- Footer (low intrusion)

**Sticky ad rules:**
- Max 3 ads visible at once
- No ads that break Core Web Vitals (lazy-loaded)
- Above-fold reserved for content (not ads)

---

### Stream 2: Affiliate Marketing (20% of revenue)

**High-converting affiliate placements per tool category:**

| Tool Category | Affiliate Partner | CPC/Commission |
|---------------|------------------|----------------|
| Legal tools | LegalZoom, Rocket Lawyer | $30–$60 CPA |
| Real estate | Stessa, Rentometer, Buildium | $20–$50 CPA |
| HR tools | Gusto, BambooHR, Indeed | $50–$200 CPA |
| Finance/Tax | TurboTax, QuickBooks, FreshBooks | $20–$80 CPA |
| Small business | Shopify, Stripe, Wix | $50–$150 CPA |

**Affiliate placement rules:**
- Max 2 affiliate placements per tool page
- Must be contextually relevant
- Clearly labeled as "Recommended Tools"
- A/B tested for CTR optimization

---

### Stream 3: SaaS Freemium Upsell (15% of revenue)

**Free Tier (default):**
- 10 generations/hour
- PDF exports with watermark
- 50 saved documents
- 1 workspace

**Premium Tier ($9.99/month or $79/year):**
- Unlimited generations
- Watermark-free exports
- Unlimited saved documents
- 5 workspaces
- Priority AI (faster)
- Custom branding on exports
- API access (100 calls/day)
- Email support

**Business Tier ($29.99/month or $249/year):**
- Everything in Premium
- 10 workspaces / team members
- API access (1,000 calls/day)
- White-label exports
- Priority support
- Bulk generation

---

### Stream 4: Lead Generation (5% of revenue)

**High-value lead types:**
- Business formation (→ LegalZoom, Incfile, Northwest RA)
- Payroll setup (→ Gusto, ADP, Paychex)
- Accounting software (→ QuickBooks, Xero, FreshBooks)
- Business banking (→ Mercury, Relay, Chase Business)
- Insurance (→ Next Insurance, Hiscox)

**Lead gen CTAs placed at:**
- End of legal document generation
- After financial calculator results
- In contextual tooltip/sidebar for relevant tools

## 3. RPM Optimization Strategy

**High-RPM keyword targeting:**
- Legal documents: $15–$50 RPM
- Finance/tax: $12–$35 RPM
- Real estate: $10–$25 RPM
- HR/recruitment: $8–$20 RPM

**Content strategy for RPM:**
- Long-form articles (1500+ words) = higher RPM
- Informational intent pages = higher RPM than navigational
- Pages about money topics = highest RPM

**Session duration tactics:**
- Multi-step tool flow
- "Generate Another" prompts
- Related tools sidebar
- Contextual article suggestions after generation
